Online Risk-Averse Planning in POMDPs Using Iterated CVaR Value Function

Yaacov Pariente, Vadim Indelman

Preprint, 2026

We address risk-sensitive decision-making in partially observable environments using Iterated Conditional Value-at-Risk (ICVaR). We develop a policy evaluation algorithm and extend three established online planning methods to optimize ICVaR rather than expected returns, providing finite-time performance guarantees and demonstrating reduced tail risk compared to conventional expectation-based approaches.

Yaacov Pariente, Vadim Indelman

Preprint, 2026

Accelerated Online Risk-Averse Policy Evaluation in POMDPs with Theoretical Guarantees and Novel CVaR Bounds

Yaacov Pariente, Vadim Indelman

Preprint, 2026

We address risk-averse decision-making under uncertainty in partially observable domains by deriving novel distributional bounds on Conditional Value-at-Risk (CVaR) using auxiliary random variables. We establish upper and lower bounds on the CVaR value function computable from a simplified belief-MDP, and employ action elimination to safely discard suboptimal choices while maintaining consistency with the original model, achieving substantial computational speedups.

Bounding Conditional Value-at-Risk via Auxiliary Distributions with Bounded Discrepancies

Yaacov Pariente, Vadim Indelman

Preprint, 2025

The paper proves bounds on the Conditional Value-at-Risk (CVaR), a well-known and widely used risk measure in the literature, using an auxiliary variable with known properties, enabling CVaR estimation when direct data is limited or expensive to sample. The approach provides practical bounds and concentration inequalities, offering tools for controlling tail risk in risk management and decision-making applications.

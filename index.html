About Me

I am a PhD student in Applied Mathematics at the Technion, advised by Vadim Indelman in the Autonomous Navigation and Perception Lab. I hold a B.Sc. in Mathematics and Statistics and an M.A. in Statistics, both from the Hebrew University of Jerusalem.

My research focuses on safe decision-making and performance acceleration for autonomous agents operating under uncertainty. I develop planning algorithms for Partially Observable Markov Decision Processes (POMDPs) — the mathematical framework behind real-time decision-making when an agent cannot fully observe its environment — with formal performance guarantees and applications to robotics and AI systems.

Open-Source Software

I created POMDPPlanners, an open-source Python package that unifies leading POMDP planning algorithms in a single framework — the first Python package to do so. It makes state-of-the-art POMDP planning algorithms accessible to researchers and practitioners, implements safety-critical benchmark environments, and supports large-scale distributed simulations. The package is released under a permissive license for commercial use.

Research Interests
